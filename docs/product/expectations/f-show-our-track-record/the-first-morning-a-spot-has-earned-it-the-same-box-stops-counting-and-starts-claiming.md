## Intent
The decision-13 differentiator made real: when a spot has genuinely cleared every gate — at
least ten verified pairs, five distinct trust-eligible reporters, a bias larger than twice its
floored standard error — the box stops counting and states a plain-Spanish claim with its
evidence beside it, and the printed plus-minus is the floored standard error that coordinated
liars cannot shrink. This is the most consequential surface this feature produces, which is
exactly why it cannot be rushed: the same gate that makes the claim honest makes it unearnable
at launch.

## Charter
Read the earned claim the way a regular would: does it tell me, in plain surf Spanish, how this
site actually runs at my beach, and does it show its evidence? A trustworthy claim states its
number quietly, ties it to how many reports and how many people back it, and prints a plus-minus
that respects the physical noise floor. Marketing sounds confident and hides its evidence — if
the sentence reads like an ad, that is a failure even if the number is right. Then tour sub-gate
beaches deliberately: they must still be counting, calmly, with not one claim-shaped number
anywhere. Every page shows exactly one of the two states, never both, never neither.
