# Feature context: f-forecast-learns-from-the-beach

Intent: Once enough different people have reported a spot, its forecast visibly moves toward what
people actually saw, and where the evidence is still too thin the site refuses to correct it and
says so.

Workspace opened 2026-08-09 as a DOCS-ONLY task. Recovery on 2026-08-10 found slice-01 delivery
recorded through 01-07, plus owner-authorised acceptance scaffolds and a 47-step map for slices
02-07. Those later slices are PARKED, not entered: their scenarios remain skipped and must receive
a fresh RED run at their own DELIVER open. No scaffold, synthetic fixture, or map is evidence that
the forecast has learned from real surfers.

Provenance, found versus authored: the feature-id `f-forecast-learns-from-the-beach` is derived
from epic row `F-FORECAST-LEARNS-FROM-THE-BEACH` (`docs/epic/surfs-up-panama/epic-delta.md` line
51) by the same lowercasing convention every sibling uses, verified against each sibling's
`des-feature-context-bootstrap` header. No `docs/feature/f-forecast-*` path has ever existed on
any git ref (checked `git log --all --diff-filter=A --name-only`). `BUILD-ORDER.md` and
`plan-cluster-*.md`, which the sibling workspaces were opened from, exist in no tree searched
(this worktree, every git ref, `/Users/andres/panama-surf`), so no pre-existing slice thinking
for this feature was found; the slicing below is authored here from the design SSOT,
`docs/product/architecture/06-learning-layer.md` (PROPOSED 2026-08-08, the owner file), its three
ADRs, `domain-model.md` §9 and §11, and the shipped code. The keystone already voted on this
feature's seam: its Out-of-scope row reads "Learning and correction fitting (the hook ships inert
at zero) | F-FORECAST-LEARNS-FROM-THE-BEACH", and the inert hook is on disk
(`src/scoring/engine.ts` lines 274 to 283, `src/pipeline/build.ts` line 173).

The shape of this feature, stated up front because it is unusual: ZERO surf reports exist today,
so nothing can be FITTED today. But the layer is almost entirely pure math over declared laws, and
the laws are fully specified in 06 with exact formulas, exact constants and a worked example. The
plan therefore separates three things: (1) machinery that is buildable and provable now with
property tests over synthetic inputs, slices 01 and 03 to 05; (2) the gate that keeps corrections
OFF until real data passes it, born inside slice-01 and hardened by slice-02 and slice-05, the
most safety-critical work in the feature; (3) the two rows that are genuinely blocked, slice-06 on
missing infra and slice-07 on real report volume, which no amount of building can conjure. A
sentence proven on synthetic inputs is stated as machinery, never as learning achieved.

  slice-05 (G7). Hard-blocked on data that does not exist: slice-07, and nothing else.
- **How "no correction applied" is structural, not a comment.** Four independent mechanisms, each
  its own requirement row: (1) no file exists in production and `apply(seed, absent) = seed` is
  already asserted by shipped keystone tests; (2) `applied` is a case of the shipped
  `CorrectionGate` sum type constructible only through the gate function, so an ungated
  correction cannot be represented, only refused ones can; (3) the builder re-checks the gates
  and clamps at read time, so even a corrupt or hand-forged file cannot move a number past them;
  (4) deleting every correction file reverts the product to day-0 in the next build. The fit
  emitting nothing on zero reports is the fifth, trivial, mechanism.
- **No slice ships a sentence that is not true at the moment it ships.** Slices 01 to 06 claim
  machinery and fences, all provable on fixtures at ship time; only slice-07 claims that the
  forecast moved, and it cannot ship until that is true. The worked example in 06 §11 is test
  oracle material, never product copy.

  edit); `src/scoring/engine.ts` carries the `applyCorrection` body slice-02 writes and the shipped
