# Feature context: f-bill-stays-zero-and-stays-up

Intent: The hourly ingest cannot stop in silence: Andres hears about a stall within 2 to 3 hours instead of finding a frozen forecast days later. The prediction archive cannot be destroyed by one console click. The month closes at $0.00 for this project on the AWS bill, read from the account, not hoped.

Wording note, on the record: the epic row promises stall detection "within the hour". The settled design refuses that number: `08-devops.md` §7 "Timing honesty" sets the ALARM email at roughly 2 to 3 hours after the last successful run and states that tightening it buys flapping, not signal. `system-architecture.md` §10 alarm 1 carries the same config. Every slice and charter below is written to the honest 2 to 3 hour floor. The epic row needs amending once Andres confirms (open question 1); nothing here silently overrules him.

## Wave: DISCUSS / [REF] Open questions

Decisions this workspace refuses to invent. Each names what it blocks.

1. **"Within the hour" or the 2 to 3 hour floor.** The epic F-BILL row promises within the hour; `08-devops.md` §7 sets the floor at roughly 2 to 3 hours and refuses to tighten because shorter periods against an hourly cadence buy flapping, not signal. Everything in this workspace is written to the floor. If Andres accepts the floor, the epic row needs its one-line amendment; if he overrules the design, slice-02's declared periods change and the flapping cost is his call. **Blocks** nothing mechanically today; blocks the epic row's honesty and slice-02's final declared values.
2. **Who builds the four real CDK stacks.** `system-architecture.md` §11 names `site-stack`, `ingest-stack`, `write-stack`, `observability-stack`. On disk they are placeholder declaration constants and no slice row anywhere owns realizing them. Options on record: an explicit stack-realization slice added to this feature, or each stack ships inside the feature that first needs it deployed, with a named owner for site and ingest before the first deploy. **Blocks** scheduling of slices 04 and 05, and every server-side feature's first deploy.
3. **The mechanism for the $20 last line.** `system-architecture.md` §9 guardrail 9's premise is false: there is no $20 CloudWatch billing alarm on the account to import (zero alarms verified 2026-08-09, HANDOFF §10; the only $20 budget is the other project's `agentflow-guardrail`). The line must be created. Whether it is created as a CloudWatch billing alarm in CDK or as a fourth budget line is a design call not made here; guardrail 9 and `system-architecture.md` §10 alarm 4 need rewriting either way. **Blocks** slice-03's DISTILL detail, not its shape.
4. **The project cost-allocation tag key name.** No settled decision names the key this project tags its resources with, and the key is load-bearing: slice-03 applies it to every resource and slice-05 groups Cost Explorer by it. **Blocks** slice-03's tagging detail and slice-05's per-project query.
